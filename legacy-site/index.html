Our Story · ਸਾਡੀ ਕਹਾਣੀ

A studio where fabric meets feeling.

“Libaas” means attire — but at our Sector 79 studio it means much more. Every outfit begins as a conversation over chai: your occasion, your comfort, the dupatta your grandmother wore. Then — styled by our designer Harpreet — our master artisans translate it into thread, zari and silk.

From wedding-day lehengas to everyday Punjabi suits, everything is measured on you, stitched in-house and finished by hand — nothing off a shelf, nothing repeated twice.
